Add them to your Home Screen

iPhone and iPad (Safari)
  1. Open the app above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen. Each app is added on its own, and each keeps its own storage, so sign in once in each one you use.
  4. For reminders, turn them on in Proairetos (Settings, Notifications). The other apps send theirs through it.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the app above in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it is offered, or the menu (⋮), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Long-press the icon for shortcuts, such as writing something down or logging a glass.
A computer (Chrome, Edge or Safari)
  1. Open the app above.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, choose the install icon in the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Press Ctrl or ⌘ and K in Proairetos to go anywhere or write something down.

Privacy

On your phone

Everything works without an account and without a connection. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on sync.

Encrypted if you sync

With sync on, your data is encrypted on your phone with a passphrase only you know. The server can’t read it.

No scores

No streaks, badges or rankings.
