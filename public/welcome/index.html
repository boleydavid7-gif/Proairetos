Why Proairetos

The name

Proairetos (προαιρετός) is ancient Greek for what is chosen deliberately. It comes from prohairesis (προαίρεσις), the Stoic name for our capacity to consider, judge and choose how we respond and act.

We cannot control everything that happens to us, or give equal attention to everything that asks for it. We can become more deliberate about where our attention goes, how we use our time and what we put first.

Why I made it

I wanted to make it easier to bring the moving parts of everyday life together: a place to organize responsibilities, keep track of what matters, plan ahead and make room for personal growth.

Not another system demanding your attention, but a tool that gives you more freedom to decide where your attention belongs. Because getting organized is meant to give you more time to live, not less.

You don’t have to do everything. You don’t have to plan every moment. You just have to make room for the things that matter to you.

What you can do

Capture in seconds

Type “dentist Friday 3pm” and it’s on Friday at 3 with a reminder. Not sure what something is yet? Write it down and sort it later.

Plan around your real week

See today’s plans, your to-dos and the free time between them. Days, evenings, nights or no set hours: your day starts when you say it does.

Look back

Keep a short journal, note three good things at the end of the day and record the decisions you make.

No guilt list

Unfinished things don’t turn red or pile up. Move them, keep them or let them go.

Private by default

Your data stays on your device. Sync is optional and end-to-end encrypted.

Add Proairetos to your Home Screen

Every app you add opens from Proairetos's Apps tab, and its day shows on Today.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)
  1. Open proairetos.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen. Reminders work from there.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open proairetos.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it is offered, or the menu (⋮), then Install app.
A computer (Chrome, Edge or Safari)
  1. Open proairetos.com.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, choose the install icon in the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File, then Add to Dock.
