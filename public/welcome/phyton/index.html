What it does

  • Checked, not scheduled

    Watered or still moist: the timing learns from what you actually did.

  • Identify

    Name a plant from up to three photos, with care notes for about 85 types.

  • Outside

    Rain counts as watering, and frost tonight brings a reminder.

  • Away

    A sheet for whoever waters while you are gone.

Privacy

On your phone

Everything works without an account and without a connection. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on sync.

Encrypted if you sync

With sync on, your data is encrypted on your phone with a key only you hold. The server can’t read it.

No scores

No streaks, badges or rankings.
