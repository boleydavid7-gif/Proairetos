Poreia

Trips and time off

Plan a trip from the first idea to home again: one place or several, days with their weather, bookings brought in from any confirmation, a map of what is around, packing, spending shared with the people going, and time off against your work schedule.

Open Poreia Hear when it opens

What it does

  • Days that fit

    Each day with its forecast, the walk between two places, and the stretches with nothing set.

  • Bookings from anywhere

    Paste a confirmation, or bring in a PDF, a screenshot or a calendar invite; each lands on its day with its code.

  • Together

    Share a trip with the people going: the same days, bookings, packing and spending, and who paid what.

  • Around you

    A map of each place with the guide’s picks, what is nearby and events in your dates, with directions.

  • Time off

    Your balance and how it grows, with each trip’s hours counted from your work shifts.

  • With Proairetos

    Approved time off takes those shifts off your schedule; trips show on Today and in Days ahead.

Privacy

On your phone

Everything works without an account and without a connection. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on sync.

Encrypted if you sync

With sync on, your data is encrypted on your phone with a key only you hold. The server can’t read it.

No scores

No streaks, badges or rankings.
