Poreia

Trip planning and time off

Plan a trip from the first idea to the flight home. Bookings, day plans, maps, packing and shared costs in one place, plus your time off.

Start your free trial Open Poreia

Features

  • Day by day

    Each day with its forecast, travel times between places and your free time.

  • Bookings from anywhere

    Paste a confirmation or add a PDF, screenshot or calendar invite. It lands on the right day.

  • Travel together

    Share a trip with the people going: the same plans, packing list and costs, and who paid what.

  • Around you

    A map of places to see, eat and stay, plus events during your trip, with directions.

  • Time off

    Your balance and how it grows, with each trip’s hours counted from your work schedule.

  • With Proairetos

    Time off clears those shifts from your schedule, and trips show on your Today.
