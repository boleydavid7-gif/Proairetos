Add it to your Home Screen

iPhone and iPad (Safari)
  1. Open proairetos.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen. Reminders work from there.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open proairetos.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it is offered, or the menu (⋮), then Install app.
A computer (Chrome, Edge or Safari)
  1. Open proairetos.com.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, choose the install icon in the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File, then Add to Dock.
