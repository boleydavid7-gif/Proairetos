Noesis

Read your books. Keep what stays with you

A reading app for the books you own and borrow. It opens EPUB and PDF files in your browser, keeps your highlights and notes in one place you can search, and starts you on rabbit holes from any question.

Subscribe · $6.99/mo Open Noesis

Features

  • Read what you own

    EPUB and PDF books in a reader with your choice of type, spacing and theme, and a read-aloud voice.

  • Keep what you read

    Highlight a passage and add a thought. Everything lands in one place you can search, and Kindle highlights come in.

  • Rabbit holes

    Start from a question. Each one brings books, articles and videos, and you go deeper one question at a time.

  • Ask Noema

    Ask about a passage, a chapter, a book or your whole library. Answers point back to your own pages.

  • Your books, everywhere

    Keep books in your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox and open them on every device.

  • Library loans

    See whether your library has a book on the shelf, and keep due dates with a link to renew.

Works on every device

Noesis is a premium app, $6.99 a month, billed through your Proairetos account and separate from the free trial. It works without an account for reading; sign in to sync. It opens in any browser, and installs to your Home Screen on iPhone, Android or a computer.

Privacy

On your phone

Works without an account or a connection. Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on sync.

Encrypted if you sync

Your data is encrypted on your device with a key only you have. We can’t read it.

No scores or streaks

No badges, points or rankings.
